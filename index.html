AI Content Factory

AI Content Factory is an automated short-form video creation and publishing project powered by n8n.

How It Works

The application processes content requests, generates video scripts, creates video assets, assembles final videos, and supports publishing to selected social media platforms through their authorized integrations.

TikTok Integration

The planned TikTok integration allows an authorized account holder to connect their TikTok account and publish generated videos using TikTok's official Content Posting API, subject to the permissions and approval granted by TikTok.

Contact

For application-related questions, contact the project operator using the contact information provided during application registration.
