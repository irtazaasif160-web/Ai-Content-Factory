Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. About AI Content Factory

AI Content Factory is an operator-managed automation application powered by n8n. It is designed to generate, process, store, and publish short-form video content. The application is currently intended for the operator's own authorized social media accounts and does not offer public user registration.

2. Information Processed

Depending on the enabled workflow features, AI Content Factory may process the following information:

  • Content requests and video topics
  • Generated scripts and video prompts
  • Generated video and audio assets
  • Video titles, captions, descriptions, and hashtags
  • Authorized social media account information
  • API authorization credentials and tokens
  • Publishing results and workflow records

3. How Information Is Used

Information is processed for the following purposes:

  • Generating video scripts and content
  • Creating and assembling short-form videos
  • Storing project assets
  • Connecting authorized social media accounts
  • Publishing content to selected platforms
  • Recording workflow and publishing results
  • Maintaining and troubleshooting the application

4. Third-Party Services

AI Content Factory uses n8n for workflow automation. Depending on the enabled features, information may also be processed by connected third-party services, including:

  • Google Sheets
  • Google Drive
  • DeepSeek
  • ElevenLabs
  • KIE.ai
  • Shotstack
  • YouTube
  • Instagram
  • TikTok

These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies and applicable agreements.

5. TikTok Integration

When TikTok integration is enabled and authorized, AI Content Factory uses TikTok's official API to perform approved publishing functions. Access is limited to the permissions granted through TikTok's authorization process. AI Content Factory does not request the TikTok account password.

6. Data Storage and Security

Project records and generated assets may be stored in connected Google services and other configured workflow services. Access to application credentials and stored information is restricted to the project operator and authorized service integrations. Reasonable measures are taken to protect credentials and prevent unauthorized access.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Project information is retained for as long as necessary to operate and maintain AI Content Factory, subject to applicable service requirements. The operator may delete stored project records and generated assets from services under their control. Third-party platforms may retain information according to their own policies.

For questions about stored information or deletion requests, contact the email address provided below.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features, integrations, or data-handling practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page.
